The Lie You're Believing The belief keeping the gap open

The Pattern Underneath What's really driving it
You're not preparing. You're hiding in preparation.

The studying feels productive. It is productive, in isolation. But when it becomes the default response to fear, when every time selling feels uncomfortable you reach for a new framework, it stops being self-development and starts being self-protection. You're not growing your business. You're growing your knowledge base. Those are not the same thing.

You'll finish reading this, feel a strange mix of seen and defensive, and then go open the next module in a program you're halfway through. That's okay. I'm not here to shame the studying.
